New in this update:

• Qubble now speaks 56 languages — 54 of them new, from Spanish and Czech to Hindi, Chinese and Arabic. It follows your phone's language, or pick one in Settings.
• During a combo the score no longer breaks across lines.
• Game over, skins, daily calendar, puzzle levels and feedback now fit small phones and large text.
• Achievements now pay out: coins, and at the top of each category an animated block skin.
