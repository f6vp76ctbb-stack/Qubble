New in this update:

• Qubbies: pets that play along. After three runs you choose your first one, and it costs nothing. It sits on your board and flies along every line you clear.
• Fairer starts: runs last longer.
• Row effects: clear rows with laser, fire, frost or lightning.
• Achievements now come in five stages, each with a reward.
• Puzzle mode: take a hint when you are stuck.
• A new game-over screen.
