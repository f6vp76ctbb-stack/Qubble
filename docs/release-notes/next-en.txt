New in this update:

• Qubble now speaks 18 languages: Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Polish, Dutch, Ukrainian, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Chinese, Arabic and Hindi join English and German. It follows your phone's language, or pick one in Settings.
• During a combo the score no longer breaks across lines.
• Game over, skins, daily calendar, puzzle levels and feedback now fit small phones and large text.
• Theme and skin names are translated.
