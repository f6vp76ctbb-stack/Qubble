2. Overview

Qubble is a block puzzle you can play without an account and without internet. Your progress is stored on your device. Data goes to others only in these cases:

  • Ads: optional bonus videos through Google AdMob, with a consent form (sections 4 and 5).
  • Statistics and crash reports: only if you agree (section 6).
  • Leaderboard: only if you choose a name (section 7).
  • Purchases through Google Play (section 9), feedback when you write to us (section 10), and the website hosted by GitHub (section 11).

The web version in the browser shows no ads and sends no statistics; only sections 3, 7, 10 and 11 apply to it.

3. Storage on your device

The app stores on your device: best scores, coins, diamonds, streaks, unlocked designs, purchase status, settings, and your answers to the questions about ads and statistics. In the web version this is kept in your browser's storage.

Legal basis: Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), because the game cannot work without this storage; to the extent it is personal data, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Duration: until you uninstall the app or (web) clear the site data; "Reset progress" deletes the progress but keeps purchases and settings. If backup is switched on on your Android device, Android backs this data up to your Google account; you control that in the Android settings, and we have no access to it.

4. Consent form for ads (Google UMP)

At start-up the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether consent for ads is required in your region (for example in the EU) and then shows the form. Google processes your IP address and device information for this. Your answer is stored on the device. You can change it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR (obtaining and demonstrating consent).

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Qubble shows no forced ads. The only ad format is rewarded videos that you start yourself. They come from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Dublin, Ireland). After the form in section 4, the AdMob SDK starts and loads videos in advance so that one is ready when you tap. Google processes device and advertising identifiers (such as the advertising ID), app and device information, interactions with ads, and an approximate location derived from the IP address.

  • Personalised ads only with your consent: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG.
  • Without consent Google shows only non-personalised or limited ads. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in funding the free game through optional videos (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Duration: according to Google's retention periods. policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps

6. Statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Only with your consent. On first start the app asks whether it may send usage statistics and crash reports. If you say yes, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) send to Google: events in the game (such as rounds started, bonus videos, a purchase as an event with the product ID), device information (model, operating system, app version), a country derived from the IP address, a random app identifier and, after a crash, the error report with the game state (mode, level, number of moves). Your leaderboard name is never included. The advertising signals in Firebase are switched off: this data is not used for advertising.

  • Purpose: finding and fixing crashes, improving the game.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG.
  • Withdrawal: at any time under Settings → Statistics & crash reports. The app then deletes the analytics data on the device and discards crash reports that have not been sent.
  • Duration: event data for at most 14 months (a Google Analytics setting), crash reports for 90 days (according to Firebase). firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Older versions: up to version 1.5.2 the app did not ask and sent this data from start-up. From version 1.5.3 it sends it only with your consent; please update the app.

7. Leaderboard (Google Firebase)

The leaderboard is optional. If you choose a name, your name, your best scores (endless mode, Daily Challenge, puzzle stars) and an anonymous technical identifier are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and shown publicly to all players. So that each name exists only once, the name is recorded as taken together with the identifier. No account, email address or real name is needed; please do not choose a name that identifies you. Even when you only view the leaderboard, the app loads it from Google, which transmits your IP address.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the leaderboard you take part in); for the identifier stored on the device, Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.
  • Duration: until you delete your entry (Settings → Delete leaderboard entry, also possible by email). Name, scores and record are then deleted. The anonymous identifier itself remains in Firebase Authentication, without name or scores; on request we delete it too (tell us your name before you delete the entry).
  • Moderation: you can report offensive names in the leaderboard or hide them for yourself. Reported names that break the rules are removed.

8. Google Play Games (Android, currently switched off)

A connection to Google Play Games is prepared but switched off in the current version. Once a version uses it: if you are signed in to Play Games on your device, the Play Games SDK signs you in at start-up, and the app sends unlocked achievements, scores and the daily streak to your Play Games profile. Google processes your Play Games player name, your avatar and analytics and diagnostic data. Who can see this is set in your Play Games profile. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Details from Google

9. In-app purchases

All purchases are optional and go through Google Play. Only Google processes payment data; we receive order information (such as order number, product, price, country) but no payment data. The app stores that a product was bought and reports the purchase as an event to Firebase if you allowed statistics (section 6).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for accounting records Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Duration: as long as tax and commercial law require these records to be kept.

10. Feedback and contact

By email (also from within the app, for example feedback or reporting a name): we process your email address, your message and the attached app version and device type in order to reply and fix problems. The mailbox is hosted by Microsoft (Outlook.com). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling your request); for questions about a purchase, point (b). We delete the message once the request is settled.

As a GitHub issue (optional): this needs a GitHub account, and the issue is public. The provider is GitHub, Inc. (USA); the GitHub privacy statement applies.

11. Website and web version (GitHub Pages)

These pages and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open them, GitHub processes your IP address and technical details (such as browser and time) to deliver and secure the pages. We have no access to these logs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure and reliable delivery). The pages embed no fonts, scripts or trackers from other providers; only the leaderboard loads from Google (section 7).

12. Reminders, sharing, ratings

Reminders are optional and scheduled on the device; nothing is sent to us for them. Sharing uses your device's share menu. When the app asks for a rating, Google Play shows the rating window; only Google receives the rating.

13. Recipients and transfers to the USA

Recipients are Google (Google Ireland Limited; Google LLC, USA), GitHub, Inc. (USA) and Microsoft (email). Transfers to the USA are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework of 10 July 2023; Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. are certified under it (dataprivacyframework.gov).

14. Children

Qubble can be played at any age but is not directed at children. Anyone under 16 needs a parent's approval for consents (ads, statistics) (Art. 8 GDPR).

15. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), see sections 4 to 6. Write to thinkube@outlook.de. As we only know the data under an anonymous identifier, please give your leaderboard name with requests about the leaderboard.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing the data unless there are compelling legitimate grounds.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us: the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (Germany).

16. Obligation to provide data, automated decisions

You do not have to provide any data. Without consent there are no personalised ads and no statistics, without a name no leaderboard; you can always play. No automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

17. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The version at this address applies.
